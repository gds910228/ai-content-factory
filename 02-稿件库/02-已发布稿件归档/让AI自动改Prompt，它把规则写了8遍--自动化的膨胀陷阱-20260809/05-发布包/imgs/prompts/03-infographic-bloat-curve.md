Hand-drawn educational infographic on warm cream paper (#F5F0E8). Black hand-drawn ink lines (#1A1A1A) with slight wobble. Soft pastel block fills: Light Blue #A8D8EA, Mint #B5E5CF, Lavender #D5C6E0, Peach #FFD5C2. Coral Red #E8655A for ONE emphasis only. Diagram-style, never realistic, generous white space. ALL text hand-lettered Chinese, no computer fonts, short labels only. Do not render color names or hex codes as visible text.

Title (top, bold oversized hand-lettered): 一份提示词怎么被养胖的

Center: a hand-drawn bar-chart / rising curve going left to right across four stages. Four pastel bars of increasing height, each a different pastel fill, labeled under the axis: 初版 (~百行), +1轮失败 (两三百行), 又两轮 (四五百行), 绕回原点 (又涨一截). A wavy hand-drawn curve sweeps upward over the bars. From the top of the fourth bar, a bold curved hand-drawn arrow loops back leftward to the first bar, labeled 绕回原点. Beside the chart two small annotation callouts: 同规则散布 8 处, and 膨胀 2.75 倍 - the 膨胀 2.75 倍 callout is circled in Coral Red, the only coral element.

Bottom takeaway (hand-lettered): 每次一小段，累积好几倍

Aspect 16:9. Large, legible, hand-lettered Chinese. Clean composition, the rising bars and return arrow centered and clearly readable.
