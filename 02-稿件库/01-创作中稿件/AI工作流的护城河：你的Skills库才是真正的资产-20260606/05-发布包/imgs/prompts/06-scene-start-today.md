Hand-drawn warm educational scene on warm cream paper. Black hand-drawn lines with
slight wobble, soft pastel color blocks. Feels friendly, motivating, easy to
understand. Diagram-style / sketchnote visuals ONLY — no photorealistic images.

PALETTE: macaron — soft pastel on warm cream
COLORS: Warm Cream background (#F5F0E8); Black (#1A1A1A) for ALL lines, text,
        figures, arrows; soft pastel accents in Mint Green (#B5E5CF) and Light Blue
        (#A8D8EA); Peach (#FFD5C2) for the warm "first skill" highlight. Color
        values are rendering guidance only — do NOT display color names or hex
        codes as visible text.

FOCAL POINT: A simple stick-figure person on a small journey from a tired
"want-to-be-lazy" moment to a bright "create my first Skill" moment.

LAYOUT (left → right), a short three-beat sequence with wavy hand-drawn arrows
between beats:
- Beat 1 (left): the stick figure slumped at a desk cluttered with sticky notes,
  a thought bubble reading "这活儿要是能自动化就好了…". A small clock doodle.
- Beat 2 (middle): the same figure now sitting up, a lightbulb doodle above the
  head, opening a blank document with a pencil. Tag "花一两小时".
- Beat 3 (right): the figure smiling, holding up a finished "Skill #1" card/file
  with a star, a small upward "更快一倍" arrow beside it. Highlight in Peach.

- TOP: Bold hand-lettered Chinese title "从今天最想偷懒的任务开始", oversized,
       slightly wobbly, with a small decorative underline.
- BOTTOM: One short hand-lettered takeaway sentence: "你的第一个 Skill，就从这里开始".

ELEMENTS: Stick-figure character (consistent across three beats), desk/sticky-
          notes, thought bubble, lightbulb, pencil, document, "Skill #1" card,
          star, upward arrow, wavy hand-drawn connector arrows, doodle decorations
          (stars, underlines) sparing.

STYLE: Minimal, well-organized, airy. Color fills don't completely fill outlines.

Human figures: simplified stylized silhouettes, not photorealistic.
Text large and prominent with handwritten-style fonts, focus on keywords. All text
in Chinese.
