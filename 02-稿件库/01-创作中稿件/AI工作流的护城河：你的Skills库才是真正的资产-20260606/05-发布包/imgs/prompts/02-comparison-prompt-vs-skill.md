Hand-drawn educational comparison sketchnote on warm cream paper. Black hand-drawn
lines with slight wobble, soft pastel color blocks. Feels simple, friendly, easy to
understand at a glance. Diagram-style visuals ONLY — no realistic or photographic
images.

PALETTE: macaron — soft pastel on warm cream
COLORS: Warm Cream background (#F5F0E8); Black (#1A1A1A) for ALL outlines, text,
        figures, arrows; Left card fill Coral Red tint (#FFD5C2) for the
        "consumable" side; Right card fill Mint Green (#B5E5CF) for the "asset"
        side; Coral Red (#E8655A) sparingly for the left-side warning markers.
        Color values are rendering guidance only — do NOT display color names or
        hex codes as visible text.

LAYOUT: Left | Right split with a wavy hand-drawn vertical divider down the middle.
        Hand-lettered "Prompt" label (top-left) and "Skill" label (top-right),
        both oversized and slightly wobbly.

LEFT SIDE — "Prompt（消耗品）":
- A delivery-rider stick figure being given verbal directions by another figure
  (speech bubble: "左转→右转→红色大门"). A second new rider figure stands
  nearby with empty speech bubble, waiting to be told again.
- A hand-lettered tag "用完就没" and a small burning/fading arrow icon implying
  one-time use.
- Bulleted pain notes in handwritten text: "每次重说一遍", "换个对话就丢".

RIGHT SIDE — "Skill（资产）":
- A hand-drawn MAP pinned on a wall/door that multiple stick figures can all read.
- A hand-lettered tag "越用越值钱" and a small growing-upward arrow icon.
- Bulleted notes in handwritten text: "谁来都懂", "随时可更新".

BRIDGE: A curved hand-drawn arrow bridging left → right at the bottom, with a small
        inline hand-lettered label "Skills = AI 的入职手册".

BOTTOM: Single-line hand-lettered tagline: "别再口头指路，给 AI 留张地图".

ELEMENTS: Rounded cards with dashed/solid borders, wavy hand-drawn arrows, simple
          stick-figure characters, map/doodle icons, small doodle decorations
          (stars, underlines) used sparingly.

STYLE: Minimal, well-organized, airy. Color fills don't completely fill outlines.
       ALL text hand-lettered — no computer fonts. Short labels only. Generous
       white space. Clean composition. Simple background.

Human figures: simplified stylized silhouettes, not photorealistic.
Text large and prominent with handwritten-style fonts, focus on keywords. All text
in Chinese.

ASPECT: 16:9, medium complexity.
