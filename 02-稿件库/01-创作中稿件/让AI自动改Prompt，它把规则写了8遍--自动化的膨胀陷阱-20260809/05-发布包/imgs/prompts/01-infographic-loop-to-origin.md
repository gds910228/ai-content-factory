Hand-drawn educational infographic on warm cream paper (#F5F0E8). Black hand-drawn ink lines (#1A1A1A) with slight wobble. Soft pastel block fills: Light Blue #A8D8EA, Mint #B5E5CF, Lavender #D5C6E0, Peach #FFD5C2. Coral Red #E8655A for ONE emphasis only. Diagram-style, never realistic or photographic, generous white space. ALL text hand-lettered Chinese, no computer fonts, short labels only. Do not render color names or hex codes as visible text.

Title (top, bold oversized hand-lettered): 改了四轮，绕回原点

Center: a hand-drawn circular loop laid out clockwise with four rounded pastel stage cards at 12/3/6/9 o'clock positions - 第一阶段, 第二阶段, 第三阶段, 第四阶段 - connected by wavy hand-drawn arrows flowing around the ring. Each stage card holds a small document doodle that grows visibly thicker/messier from 第一阶段 (thin, clean) to 第四阶段 (fat, tangled, covered in extra scribbled lines). On the 第四阶段 card, a bold hand-drawn curved arrow loops all the way back to 第一阶段, labeled 回到原点 - this return arrow and its label are the ONLY Coral Red elements. Along the ring, a tiny simplified stick-figure "diligent worker" doodle cheerfully adding small color blocks onto each document, eyes happy, oblivious.

Bottom takeaway (hand-lettered): 自动化越勤勉，膨胀越静音

Aspect 16:9. Large, legible, hand-lettered Chinese. Clean composition, generous white space, the loop centered and clearly readable. Human figures: simplified stylized silhouettes, not photorealistic.
