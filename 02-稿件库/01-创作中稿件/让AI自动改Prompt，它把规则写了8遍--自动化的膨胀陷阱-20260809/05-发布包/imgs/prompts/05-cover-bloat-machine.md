Ultra-wide hand-drawn headline cover banner on warm cream paper (#F5F0E8), 21:9 (targeting a 900x383 WeChat cover). Black hand-drawn ink lines (#1A1A1A) with slight wobble. Soft pastel accents: Light Blue #A8D8EA, Mint #B5E5CF, Lavender #D5C6E0, Peach #FFD5C2. Coral Red #E8655A used ONLY on the "?" mark. Diagram-style, never realistic, generous negative space. ALL text hand-lettered Chinese, no computer fonts. Do not render color names or hex codes as visible text.

Left third: a hand-drawn "bloat machine" - a box with simple gears and a chute. A small clean thin Prompt document is fed into the left side of the machine; from the right side a fat, tangled, overly long document puffs out, with a bold hand-drawn curved arrow looping back from the fat document's end to its start, labeled 绕回原点. Above the fat document floats a large Coral Red "?" - the only coral element.
Right two-thirds: large bold hand-lettered main title (the most visually dominant element): 你把 Prompt 维护外包给 AI. Beneath it, a smaller hand-lettered subtitle: 等于亲手养了台膨胀机器.
Top-left: a small rounded pastel badge hand-lettered: Prompt 工程系列 ④.
A few spare star/sparkle doodles scattered in the negative space for warmth.

Aspect 21:9. The main title must be the largest, most prominent element.
