Create a Xiaohongshu (Little Red Book) style infographic following these specifications.

TYPE: Infographic — Data Visualization (cover card)
ASPECT: 3:4 portrait (vertical), 2k quality

## Core Principles

- Clean composition with generous white space (60-70% of canvas). Simple background. Main elements centered.
- Hand-drawn quality throughout — diagram-style visuals ONLY, NO realistic or photographic elements.
- Color values and color names are rendering guidance only — do NOT display color names, hex codes, or palette labels as visible text in the image.

## Text Style (CRITICAL)

- ALL text MUST be hand-drawn hand-lettered style, large and prominent. NO computer-generated fonts.
- Main title characters must span at least 70% of the card width, bold and eye-catching.

## Language

- All text in Simplified Chinese, except the brand name "Xpert" which must be spelled exactly as "Xpert" in Latin letters.

## Palette (notion, default)

- Background: pure white / off-white
- Ink: black and dark gray for ALL lines, text, arrows, doodles
- Accents: light pastel blue, light pastel yellow, light pastel pink color blocks only (never render color names or codes as text)
- Style: simple line doodles with slight hand-drawn wobble, small stick figures, single-weight ink lines, clean uncluttered notion knowledge-card look

## Layout (Sparse cover, big-number list style)

- Single focal point centered, breathing room on all sides
- Nothing critical in the bottom 10% zone (the small hashtag at bottom-right is the only exception, placed just above the bottom edge)

## Content (LABELS — render ONLY these three text elements, nothing else)

1. Main title, large bold hand-lettered Chinese, centered, two lines, width ≥70% of card:
   「在 Xpert 做任务的
   5 个坑」
   — the number 「5」 rendered extra large as the visual anchor of the card
2. Small handwritten label below the title:
   「20多期亲历 · 替你踩过了」
3. Small handwritten hashtag at the bottom-right corner:
   「#Xpert」

## Visual Concept

- A cute hand-drawn stick figure tripping over a small stone doodle beside the title (friendly, humorous, single-weight ink lines)
- A soft pastel yellow highlighter swipe behind 「5 个坑」
- A few hand-drawn sparkle and small warning-sign doodles scattered sparsely

## Absolute Prohibitions

- Render ONLY the exact text listed above — absolutely NO other characters, letters, numbers, symbols, watermarks, color codes, or gibberish text anywhere on the card
- NO currency symbols, NO money amounts, NO QR codes, NO logos, NO URLs
- NO recruitment wording such as 报名, 加群, 私信
- Do not distort or drop any Chinese character; "Xpert" must be spelled exactly
