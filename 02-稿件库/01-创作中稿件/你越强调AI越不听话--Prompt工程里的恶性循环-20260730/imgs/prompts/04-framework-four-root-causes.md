# 4个底层原因 - 框架图

Hand-drawn educational framework on warm cream paper. Slight wobble on all lines.
Diagram-style visuals ONLY - no realistic or photographic images.

STRUCTURE: 2×2 matrix of four rounded concept cards, each one pastel fill, with
a short hand-lettered title and one tiny concept sketch inside.

- TOP: Bold hand-lettered title "为什么越强调越崩：4个底层原因".
- Card ① (top-left, Light Blue #A8D8EA): "U形注意力"
   tiny sketch: a U-shaped curve, peaks marked at both ends, a dip in the middle
   with a small "遗忘" note pointing to the dip.
- Card ② (top-right, Mint #B5E5CF): "静默择一"
   tiny sketch: two short rule lines, one crossed out, an arrow picking the other.

   tiny sketch: a few stacked layers, the bottom ones fading / being pushed out
   the side with small arrows.

COLORS: Warm Cream background (#F5F0E8); Black (#1A1A1A) for ALL lines, text,
